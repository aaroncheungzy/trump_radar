I hope the Public is watching as the Department of the Interior, Doug Burgum, and myself, are opening up many of the decades old, closed Fountains throughout the D.C. area. So far, over 20 have been revitalized, and fixed, looking better than the day they were built, many years ago. We have some left, some were in very bad and difficult condition, but we will get them all done in a short time. D.C. is being reawakened as to its Beauty, Elegance, and Charm. The “Granddaddy” of them all will be The Reflecting Pool — 2,500 feet long, and almost 200 feet wide, the biggest such structure ever built, but also, the most troublesome for many Administrations in that, from the time it was built in 1922, it essentially never really worked! It leaked from all angles, drew dirt, grime, and decay, was often filled with garbage, and wasn’t at all representative of the two Great Monuments it connects — The Lincoln Memorial, and The Washington Monument. But, very soon, before July 4th, it will be far better than it has ever been, even going back to as long ago as 1922 — Its Original Opening! The Biden and Obama Administrations spent over 100 Million Dollars, and made it far worse! It was only open for a short while, during each Administration, before the leaks and decay took over. I, together with Doug Burgum and the Department of Interior, am fixing it the right and proper way — It will last for many decades into the future. We’ve had rain delays, and have substantially increased our scope of work as to quality, durability, and even including the stone and granite on the outside of the water itself — A big job, much bigger than originally conceived, but it is being done, after over a Century of “stop and go’s,” correctly! You will see something very special at the Reflecting Pool in a short period of time. Stay tuned. In the meantime, watch as the Fountains open throughout D.C. Fountains go up, and Crime goes down! Also, watch for Lafayette Park, directly in front of the Main Entrance to the White House. I have made a major contribution toward it. The Fountains are now working, and look magnificent as the Park is being entirely rebuilt, using far better and more beautiful materials than originally used. As the Entrance to the White House, it’s got to be spectacular — There is no other way! Again, check out what’s going on at Lafayette Park. President DONALD J. TRUMP

我希望公众能够关注内政部、道格·布尔古姆和我本人正在开放整个华盛顿地区许多已有数十年历史、关闭的喷泉。到目前为止，已有 20 多座建筑得到了修复和修复，看起来比多年前建成时更好。我们还剩下一些，有些情况非常糟糕和困难，但我们会在短时间内把它们全部完成。华盛顿特区的美丽、优雅和魅力正在被重新唤醒。其中的“祖父”将是倒影池——长 2,500 英尺，宽近 200 英尺，是有史以来建造的最大的此类结构，但也是对许多政府来说最麻烦的，因为从 1922 年建成以来，它基本上从未真正发挥过作用！它从各个角度漏水，沾满灰尘、污垢和腐烂，经常充满垃圾，根本不能代表它所连接的两座伟大的纪念碑——林肯纪念堂和华盛顿纪念碑。但是，很快，在 7 月 4 日之前，它将比以往任何时候都好得多，甚至可以追溯到 1922 年——它最初的开幕！拜登和奥巴马政府花费了一亿多美元，雪上加霜！在每次政府执政期间，它只开放了很短一段时间，然后就出现了泄漏和腐烂。我与道格·布尔古姆和内政部一起，正在以正确和适当的方式修复它——它将在未来持续数十年。我们因下雨而延误，并大幅增加了我们在质量、耐用性方面的工作范围，甚至包括了水体外部的石头和花岗岩——这是一项艰巨的工作，比最初设想的要大得多，但经过一个多世纪的“走走停停”，它正在正确地完成！您很快就会在倒影池看到一些非常特别的东西。敬请关注。与此同时，看着整个华盛顿特区的喷泉开放。喷泉上升，犯罪下降！另外，请注意位于白宫正门正前方的拉斐特公园。我为此做出了重大贡献。喷泉现在正在工作，而且看起来很宏伟，因为公园正在完全重建，使用的材料比原来使用的更好、更漂亮。作为白宫的入口，它必须非常壮观——没有其他办法！再次，看看拉斐特公园发生了什么。唐纳德·J·特朗普总统
